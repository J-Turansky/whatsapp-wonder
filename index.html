About WhatsApp Wonder

WhatsApp Wonder turns an exported WhatsApp chat into stats, fun facts and a story — entirely inside your browser. It runs 100% in-browser: there are no uploads, no tracking and no AI services involved.

  1. In WhatsApp, open the chat, then choose ⋮ / contact name → More → Export chat → Without media.
  2. Unzip the export if needed, then choose the _chat.txt file here or paste its text.
  3. Check the import confirmation, then explore Stats, Awards and Story. All analysis stays in this browser session.
